# Course Overview

Generative AI has rapidly emerged as a powerful class of models capable of producing text,
code, images, and structured outputs.

In automation and robotics, however, generative AI cannot be treated as a replacement for
control logic, perception pipelines, or safety mechanisms.

This course focuses on:
- Understanding generative models in engineering contexts
- Identifying appropriate integration points
- Evaluating risks, constraints, and failure modes
- Designing human-in-the-loop and supervisory architectures

The goal is to enable students to **reason about generative AI as part of a system**, not as an isolated capability.
