# Generative AI for Automation and Robotics

This course introduces Generative AI from the perspective of **automation and robotics systems**.

Rather than treating generative models as standalone tools, the course positions them as
**decision-support and intelligence components** within larger engineered systems.

The emphasis is on understanding *where generative AI adds value*, *where it does not*, and
*how it must be constrained in safety- and reliability-critical environments*.

---

## Course Orientation

- Engineering-first perspective
- System-level integration
- Research and deployment awareness
- Strong emphasis on limitations and boundaries
