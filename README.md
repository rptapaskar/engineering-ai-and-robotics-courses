# Engineering AI and Robotics Courses

This repository documents **conceptual frameworks, system perspectives, and research-aligned structures**
for courses taught in the domains of Artificial Intelligence, Automation, and Robotics.

The intent is not to host lecture slides or day-to-day teaching material, but to:
- Frame how complex topics are taught
- Align coursework with research and industry practice
- Provide reusable conceptual scaffolding for advanced learners

This repository complements formal LMS platforms by focusing on **thinking structures**, not logistics.

---

## Scope

The courses represented here emphasize:
- AI as a system component, not a standalone tool
- Robotics and automation as integrated cyber-physical systems
- Engineering constraints such as safety, latency, interpretability, and deployment feasibility
- Research-readiness and problem formulation skills

- Generative AI for Automation and Robotics  
- AI for Mobile Robots

## Intended Audience

- Undergraduate and postgraduate engineering students  
- Research-oriented learners and project teams  
- Educators designing AI & Robotics curricula  
- Industry collaborators interested in workforce readiness  

---

## Design Philosophy

- Concepts over content volume  
- Systems over tools  
- Research alignment over trend following  
- Depth over coverage  

---

> This repository reflects how AI and Robotics education is structured to produce **system thinkers and applied researchers**.
