# Concept Map

This course is organized around the following core concepts:

---

## Generative AI Foundations
- Generative vs predictive AI
- Foundation models
- Pre-trained models and inference

---

## Interaction and Prompting
- Prompt engineering as an interface
- Prompt structure and intent
- Sensitivity to ambiguity

---

## Modalities Covered
- Text generation and reasoning
- Audio generation and analysis
- Image generation and understanding
- Introductory exposure to video generation

---

## Engineering Concerns
- Hallucination and uncertainty
- Interpretability and trust
- Human-in-the-loop supervision

---

## Responsible Use
- Ethical considerations
- Domain suitability
- Safety awareness

The course emphasizes **relationships between concepts**
rather than internal model mechanics.
