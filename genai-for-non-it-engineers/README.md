# Generative AI for Non-IT Engineers

This course introduces Generative AI for **non-IT engineering disciplines**
such as Automation & Robotics, Mechanical, Civil, Chemical, Biotechnology,
and allied domains.

The course emphasizes **conceptual clarity, system integration, and responsible use**
of generative AI rather than low-level model development.

Programming and tools are used as **means of exploration**, not as end goals.

---

## Course Orientation

- Designed for non-IT engineering students and faculty
- Focus on confidence-building and correct mental models
- Emphasis on *where* GenAI fits in engineering systems
- Strong attention to limitations, risks, and boundaries

---

## What This Course Is

- An introduction to GenAI as an **engineering capability**
- A guided exploration of text, audio, image, and video models
- A foundation for domain-specific GenAI projects

## What This Course Is Not

- Not a deep learning or model-training course
- Not a software engineering specialization
- Not a replacement for control, perception, or safety logic

---

## Learning Philosophy

Understanding **when and how to use GenAI** is more important than
understanding how models are trained.

---

> This course aims to produce engineers who can **reason correctly**
> about Generative AI in real engineering contexts.
