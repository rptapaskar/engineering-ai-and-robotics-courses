# Boundaries and Limitations

Generative AI systems exhibit characteristics that must be carefully considered
in engineering applications.

---

## Key Limitations
- Non-deterministic outputs
- Lack of ground truth awareness
- Hallucination under ambiguity
- Limited reliability for real-time use

---

## Implications for Non-IT Engineers
- GenAI outputs must be validated
- Human supervision is essential
- Not all problems are suitable for GenAI solutions

---

## Course Emphasis

Learners are trained to recognize:
- When GenAI is appropriate
- When it must be constrained
- When it should not be used at all
